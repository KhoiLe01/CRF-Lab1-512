SVM-MC (multiclass SVM, independent letters) sits at ~0.70 letter accuracy and ~0.17 word accuracy, and is flat or slightly worse as C increases. If the average word is about 5 letters, then even a decent per-letter accuracy gets poor results for word accuracy when we equire every letter to be correct. Roughly, 0.70^5 ≈ 0.17, which is exactly what we see. SVM-MC is not using any sequence context and cannot recover from ambiguous characters.

SVM-HMM (structured SVM / max-margin with transitions) and CRF (probabilistic, log-likelihood with transitions) both jump a lot as C increases. By the time we get to C=100 or 1000, they are both around ~0.80–0.85 letter accuracy and ~0.38–0.48 word accuracy. Both are beter than SVM-MC, especially at the word level, because the structured models use the transition and neighbor context to clean up local confusions. They act like a lightweight language model over characters.
